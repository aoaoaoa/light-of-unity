Howpowerfulis thelight ofunity?

Passages from a letter of the National Spiritual Assembly of the Bahá’ís of the United States, dated Juneteenth, 2020, each paired with an echo from earlier Bahá’í writings. Three readings of the same material.

Variants

  1. FolioA scroll of coloured leaves, one per passage. Each echo hangs beneath its statement, dated in outline numerals.
  2. ConcordanceA parallel text for study. The letter faces its echoes, and a span chart shows how far back each one reaches.
  3. RecitationOne passage per screen for presenting. The statement resolves word by word, then its echo follows.
